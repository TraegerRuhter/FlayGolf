## 1. Current landscape (2024–2026): launch monitors, mats, screens/enclosures, software, build costs

### Takeaway
The home-sim market is split into tiers. At the bottom are portable radar and camera units around $600 (Garmin R10, Rapsodo MLM2PRO). Next are mid-tier photometric units (SkyTrak+, Bushnell Launch Pro / Foresight GC3, Uneekor EYE MINI). At the top are premium overhead and camera systems (Uneekor EYE XR, Foresight QuadMAX, Full Swing KIT, Trackman). Full builds run from about $1.2k to $25k+. Nearly every tier shares the same physical-accessory problems: a hole-less mat that won't take a tee, alignment of the device and the hitter, ball dots for spin, screen wear, and noise. The launch-monitor brand doesn't change any of that, which makes accessories a brand-agnostic market.

### Inferences
- Portable units like the R10, MLM2PRO, Mevo+ and Square are set up and torn down often, and many owners have no dedicated room. That keeps re-alignment and leveling a weekly pain, and it's the main reason printed stands exist.
- Accessories that don't care about the device (tees, trays, alignment, mat repair, screen protection, organization) can be sold to the whole market. Device-specific mounts need SKUs per model and can break with hardware revisions.

## 2. Recurring complaints and DIY hacks (forums, Reddit, YouTube)

### Takeaway
The most-cited pains are tees on dense or hole-less mats, losing tees, inconsistent tee height, worn hitting strips, launch-monitor alignment and leveling, monitors hit by stray shots, screen holes, noise, cold garages, low ceilings, and loose balls and clutter. Forum users explicitly ask for a ball tray that sits level with the turf and for a launch-monitor positioning jig that confirms the device is square. Both are printable.

### Cited Findings
**Tees**
- Wooden tees won't stay in dense sim mats. Workarounds include sit-on-top tees (BirTees, Carl's HotShot, which can be cut to height), tomahawk or castle tees, cutting a hole under the mat, and tying the tee to the mat with string so it doesn't get lost. — [Carl's HotShot tees (40-pack)](https://shop.carlofet.com/carls-indoor-golf-tees); [Rain or Shine BirTees](https://rainorshinegolf.com/products/birtees); [GSF "tees for my CCE mat that don't get lost"](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/build-your-own/mats-turf-and-flooring/116190-tees-for-my-cce-mat-that-don-t-get-lost?p=116224); [GSF indoor tees what do you use](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/play-golf/equipment/48354-indoor-golf-tees-what-do-you-use); [Sand Trap tethered tee thread](https://thesandtrap.com/forums/topic/95852-tethered-golf-tee-for-the-mat)
- Thin turf mats such as GolfBays "often [aren't] sturdy enough to stay up or hold the ball". — [GolfBays teeing turf mat page](https://golfbays.co.uk/products/golfbays-teeing-up-turf-mat-2m-x-1m)
- Tee choice is suspected of affecting distance readings (SkyTrak thread), and an R50 owner reported the device not picking up a ball on a tee. — [GSF tees affecting distance](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/skytrak/119963-tees-effecting-distance); [Garmin forum R50](https://forums.garmin.com/outdoor-recreation/golf/f/approach-r50/398630/r50-not-picking-up-my-ball-on-a-tee)
- Strong demand signal: an Amazon rubber tee holder for mats is tracked at about 1,000 units/month with a 4.8 rating from 429 ratings (third-party tracker, unverified). — [asinsight via search](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/golf-simulator-enclosure)

**Mat wear**
- Users cut out and inset strips, and build DIY strips (e.g., 0.25" polycarbonate cut to 18"x36" with turf on top). Vacuuming the strip before sessions is advised. A standalone strip should be set into a mat so the ball sits level with the feet. — [Carl's Place](https://carlofet.com/blog/how-to-make-or-repair-a-golf-hitting-mat); [Sim Weekly mat guide (snippet)](https://simweekly.beehiiv.com/p/your-hitting-mat-guide-and-a-giveaway); [Golfers Authority mats](https://golfersauthority.com/best-golf-hitting-mat/); [GSF DIY cheat divot mat](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/build-your-own/mats-turf-and-flooring/369501-diy-cheat-divot-mat)
- Sensor-mat wear (ProTee) needs a fix. — [GSF ProTee wear](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/protee-golf-simulator/general/385104-wear-over-time-on-the-hitting-sensor-mat-need-a-solution)
- Mat stabilization in garages (sliding or shifting). — [GSF stabilizing mat](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/golf-simulator-forum/simulator-builds/350773-garage-sim-build-questions-for-stabilizing-golf-hitting-mat)

**Ball return**
- A printed return ramp on a Fiberbuilt mat was mentioned in the 3D-printed tees thread. — [GSF what about 3D printed tees](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/play-golf/equipment/305832-what-about-3d-printed-tees)
- A "ramp at bottom of screen" thread exists. — [GSF ramp](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/golf-simulator-forum/simulator-builds/314617-ramp-at-the-bottom-of-screen)
- No detailed DIY return guide was found.

**Launch monitor alignment and protection**
- Mevo+ owners who re-set up every session say a printed stand makes roll, tilt and alignment easier. — [GSF 3D printed stand for Mevo](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/flightscope/323051-3d-printed-stand-for-mevo)
- "Launch monitor slider" thread. — [GSF](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/golf-simulator-forum/golf-simulators/344950-launch-monitor-slider?p=345183)
- A SkyTrak owner's unit took a third errant toe shot "and it had to go back for service". This led to a printed shield with foam padding. — [MakerWorld SkyTrak protector](https://makerworld.com/models/819278)

**Explicit wishlist thread ("what equipment do you wish existed")**
- Ball tray that sits level with installed turf; control box and ball tray; a positioning tray for the launch monitor with an offset element to confirm it is square to the mat; a side tray for balls and tees. — [GSF thread 340072](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/play-golf/equipment/340072-what-equipment-do-you-whish-existed-or-was-better?p=366618) (via search snippet)

**Impact screen damage**
- "1.5 years old and already has three holes". "Each winter season (4 months) has seen holes worn in each side". Overly tight screens cause holes. Back netting helps. Scuffed or marked balls wear screens faster. — [GSF how to stop impact screen wearing](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/build-your-own/enclosures/284278-how-to-stop-impact-screen-from-wearing); [GSF Impact Screen 101](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/build-your-own/enclosures/4635-impact-screen-101?p=50855); [Elite Screens DIY material](https://elitescreens.com/products/golfsim-diy-screen)
- GolfBays sells a 30x30 cm repair patch. Elite Screens says it has no repair kits for ImpactWhite. Trackman publishes a tightening guide. — [GolfBays patch](https://golfbays.co.uk/products/golfbays-impact-screen-repair-patch-30cm-x-30cm); [Trackman support](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/7064249491483-Hitting-Screen-How-To-Tighten-an-Impact-Screen)
- GolfBays sells a "wedge protection pelmet" and a foam backing layer. — [GolfBays accessories](https://golfbays.co.uk/collections/golf-hermir-aukahlutir-aukahlutir)

**Noise**
- "You won't get rid of the low frequency noise". Ceiling and floor transfer is the hardest. Mass-loaded vinyl and panels are suggested. — [GSF noise reduction](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/build-your-own/enclosures/106958-noise-reduction-does-anything-work-well); [GSF close quarters](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/golf-simulator-forum/golf-simulators/37295-simulator-noise-and-sound-issues-in-close-quarters)

**Garage temperatures**
- Mevo+ cold-temperature and projector-in-cold-garage threads. The advice is to keep the launch monitor and laptop in the house, run a dehumidifier, and keep fresh batteries. — [GSF Mevo+ cold](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/flightscope/266505-mevo-cold-temperature-question); [GSF projector winter](https://golfsimulatorforum.com/forum/golf-simulator-forum/simulator-builds/404359-projector-in-garage-winter-use-any-concerns-with-cold-temp); [Simply Golf Simulators](https://simplygolfsimulators.com/blogs/news/winter-ready-golf-sim-maintenance-tips)

**Low ceiling**
- Floor units are preferred over overhead ones. One YouTuber switched to a 7-iron-length hybrid in a 7.5 ft room. — [BenQ](https://www.benq.eu/uk-ua/golf-simulator-projector/knowledge/garage-golf-sim-setup.html); [prked low ceiling guide](https://prked.com/post/is-your-garage-ceiling-too-low-for-a-golf-simulator-the-ultimate-guide)

**Cable management**
- A basement builder used velcro tape down the post and fiber-optic HDMI for the long projector run. — [nick.blog sim electronics](https://nick.blog/tag/golf-sim)

**Putting**
- Dedicated putting add-ons (ExPutt) and alignment-ball string aids exist, which shows putting is a weak spot of camera and radar sims. — [SimSpace ExPutt RG](https://simspacegolf.com/products/exputt-rg-golf-putting-simulator); [Fairway Jockey Alignment Ball](https://fairwayjockey.com/products/alignment-ball)

### Inferences
- The tee pain has three parts: it won't stand, it gets lost, and the height isn't repeatable. Competitors (BirTees, HotShot, castle tees) address them separately. "Tethered", "height-repeatable" and "won't fly into the screen" are all angles a tee brand can own without explaining its mechanism.
- Stray-shot damage (to the launch monitor, screen edges, side walls and projector) is a cluster of cheap, printable protective parts. It also has an emotional hook: owners just spent $600–$20k on gear.

### Gaps
- Reddit r/Golfsimulator thread volume and specific posts could not be retrieved. GolfWRX and Facebook groups weren't reached. The YouTube build-video pain points (Breaking Eighty, Rick Shiels) are unverified.

## 3. Existing 3D-printed sim accessories (Etsy, Printables, MakerWorld, Thingiverse, Amazon), competition, and product ideas

### Takeaway
Printed sim accessories already exist, mostly as free files and small Etsy-style shops: launch-monitor leveling stands (about $70 retail for an R10/MLM2PRO stand), monitor shields, TPU vase-mode sim tees, ball dispensers, and novelty tee holders. Demand looks real, but the market is fragmented, with few brands and almost none with a strong aesthetic identity. A themed brand (dark "haunted country club") selling consumables like tees and dots plus "armor" and organization parts is an open lane. Patent risk exists: K2 Golf's tee design is patented.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Paid, printed sim goods today are mostly launch-monitor stands at $40–70. Free files undercut simple parts, so paid products need finish quality, multi-material printing, bundling, a brand, or features that are hard to print (inserts, magnets, lasers, weighted bases).
- No sim-specific accessory brand with a lifestyle or aesthetic identity surfaced. The vendor brands (Carl's, Rain or Shine, Shop Indoor Golf) are retailers, not culture brands.

## 4. Indoor golf venues (B2B) and sim culture gaps (leagues, course designers, content)

### Takeaway
Venues are growing fast. X-Golf has 130+ US sites at 7–8 bays each, Five Iron has 34–36+ sites and is adding smaller "Studio" and "Lounge" formats, and NGF reports 6.2M simulator users in 2023, up 73% vs 2019. No data was found on what venues spend on consumables. Online sim culture runs through GSPro and SGT, with user-built courses (OPCD/GreenKeeper), Patreon-exclusive courses, and leagues. That's a natural place for a culture-first brand to sponsor, make trophies, and make merch.

### Inferences
- B2B: venues with 7–12 bays each replace tees, dots, and screen and mat wear parts continuously. A small maker could pitch branded tees and tee holders to independent sim bars (not chains, which buy centrally). This is unvalidated, so test with 3–5 local venues before investing.
- Culture: GSPro course designers and SGT and league organizers are an under-merchandised community. Opportunities include custom trophies, "haunted" course-themed merch (e.g., Bootleggers Lair-style partnerships), and sponsoring a league's "worst round" award, which fits the anti-country-club voice.
