## RAW NOTES (batch 2)
- Zwift: Jon Mayfield hobby prototype posted on Slowtwitch forum; Eric Min found it, partnered Jan 2014; users self-organized events via shared spreadsheets before platform had event tools; "racing came from the community"; ~quarter of users competitive; live races >100k viewers; doubled annually early (founder claims) — https://www.rouleur.cc/blogs/desire-journal/eric-min-interview-why-living-in-london-drove-me-to-create-zwift-and-how-it-went-global ; https://www.zwift.com/eu/news/31481-the-early-days ; Zwift Ride hardware, June 2026 junior program (search summary, unverified primary)
- Six Zero: Dale Young (composites engineer), ~100+ backyard prototypes with his father over ~18 months, founded Nov 2022, exports to 100+ countries, team of 30 — https://pickleball.com/gear/the-six-zero-story-from-backyard-prototypes-to-the-future-of-pickleball ; https://www.exportawards.gov.au/en/stories-news-and-media/articles/emerging-exporter-winner
- Recess: founders couldn't find paddles matching "lifestyle and aesthetic" — https://pickleballcentral.com/blog/bringing-the-fun-to-pickleball-a-chat-with-recess-pickleballs-founders/ ; Maggie Brown CEO/founder per Crunchbase — https://crunchbase.com/organization/recess-pickleball
- EarthQuaker Devices: Jamie Stillman, Akron basement 2004 (some say early '05), tour manager for Black Keys, no electronics background; ~50 attempts cloning a Fuzz Face; first pedal for Pat Carney; Hoof first full production 2007; 9 employees building hundreds/week in basement — https://www.premierguitar.com/gear/builder-profile-earthquaker-devices ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EarthQuaker_Devices (co-founder credit for Julie Robbins disputed between sources)
- Bandit: Tim West, late 2020, seeded socks in Brooklyn Track Club, no paid ads, hired BTC founder for community, store on running route, weekly run club >100 — https://thecaseforbrand.substack.com/p/how-bandit-is-building-the-underground
- Tracksmith: behaves like a running club; free runs 3x/week from stores; Hare A.C. $128/yr incl. kit, member number, letter, first dibs on drops — https://thecaseforbrand.substack.com/p/how-tracksmith-built-a-cult-running ; anti-Nike approach — https://digiday.com/marketing/running-brand-tracksmith-taken-anti-nike-approach-building-brand
- GOLF evidence: Bettinardi x Malbon "Gangster Buckets" headcover limit 1/person, sold out — https://www.golfio.com/products/bettinardi-x-malbon-limited-edition-gangster-buckets-creme-plaid-headcover ; Bettinardi Malbon Wizard covers "limited quantities", members-only page — https://bettinardi.com/products/dancing-buckets-malbon-wizard-headcover-blue ; Malbon x Monzee cover sold out — https://solefeed.com/shop/malbon-golf/malbon-golf-x-monzee-headcover-malbon-x-monzee-headcover-cfm7oa
