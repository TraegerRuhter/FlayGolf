## Raw notes, batch 1 (to be put in order at the end)
- Etsy fees: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction fee (on item + shipping), 3% + $0.25 processing (US). Offsite Ads 15% (12% for shops over $10k/yr), mandatory above $10k trailing sales. Roughly 10% all-in without ads, 22-25% on ad-attributed sales — sources: marmalead, edesk (third-party)
- MakerWorld: Exclusive Points can be cashed out at $100 thresholds, reviewed manually (Bambu blog). Third-party figure of about $0.066/point. Maker's Supply commission is 3-15%.
- Kickstarter: Shottee $8,823 / $8,000 goal, 333 backers (2019). Teebot PRO $3,396 / $3,000, 96 backers.
- USPTO: trademark base fee $350 per class from Jan 18 2025. Utility basic filing fee $350/$140/$70 (large/small/micro). Provisional ~$325 large (verify), micro is 20% and small is 40%.
- Insurance: ACT Etsy plan $279/yr; Hartford GL average $810/yr; Insuranceopedia $300-1,200/yr.
- CruiseCup: about 160 Bambu A1s, $808k revenue (Makers101, citing Business Insider).
- Injection molding: Prusa €6,000 mold, €0.18 vs €0.25-0.32 per part, break-even ~80k. Formlabs: $3,600 tool, break-even 13,050. Xometry: 250-2,000.
