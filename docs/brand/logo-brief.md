# Logo commission: Flay Golf

Copy this brief and send it to the illustrator along with
[`logo-reference.png`](logo-reference.png).

---

**About the brand:** Flay Golf is a small golf brand selling 3D-printed golf
gear, starting with a tee for golf simulator mats, plus clothing. The feel is
DIY and anti-country-club: classic golf (flat caps, leather wingtips) corrupted
by something dark and beautiful.

**The concept:** a golf ball, flayed. The cover is cut in an X and its four skin
flaps are stretched along an X-shaped wooden flaying board, nailed into the wood
at the ends. Each nail stands upright with the tip of the skin pinned flat
around its base. In the center, a torn F-shaped wound in the muscle reveals a
glowing teal core. The attached image is a reference for the composition, not
final art.

**Style**

- Old anatomical engraving: fine crosshatching, like 1800s medical plates
- The mood of Hieronymus Bosch and Zdzisław Beksiński: eerie and beautiful, a
  little gory, never over the top
- Thin, leathery, torn skin, with the dimples stretched where it's pulled
- Wet, fibrous muscle
- Old, stained, nail-scarred wood
- A real, scuffed golf ball whose round shape stays readable

**Palette:** muted Beksiński tones of bone, dusty ochre, rust, sepia and deep
umber, with one small touch of teal in the core.

**Avoid**

- Anything that resembles House Bolton's flayed-man sigil from Game of Thrones:
  no human figure, no pink or red field
- Cartoon or sticker styles, and thick outlines

**Deliverables**

1. The full detailed mark
2. A simplified one- or two-color version that still reads when embroidered on
   a cap and at about 1 inch
3. A one-color version for stamping on a 3D-printed tee
4. Vector files (SVG, AI or EPS) plus high-resolution PNGs

**Rights:** full ownership and commercial rights transferred to me in writing,
because I plan to trademark the logo.

**Budget and process:** $100–300, two rounds of revisions. Please send rough
sketches before final art.
