# Flay Golf brand

## Voice

- **Leads with:** DIY ("we make our own gear") and anti-country-club (golf for
  people who don't fit the polo-shirt crowd).
- **Loud:** a supporting voice, mostly on merch.
- **Tagline to try:** "Built, not bought. No dress code."

## Look

**Country club, haunted.** Classic golf (flat caps and pageboy hats, leather
wingtips) set against dense, dreamlike, slightly grotesque art: a 1920s golfer
wandering into a Bosch painting.

References:

- Portugal. The Man album art: *American Ghetto*, and the black, white and gold
  hand-drawn head cover
- alt-J: *The Dream* (pencil sketch) and *An Awesome Wave* (river-delta photo)
- Hieronymus Bosch, the hell panel of *The Garden of Earthly Delights*
- Zdzisław Beksiński
- Transworld Snowboarding-era board graphics
- "Disorganized beauty"

Bosch is long out of copyright and can be riffed on directly. Beksiński and the
album covers are still protected: mood only, never copied.

## How dark

- Baseline is **beautiful-dark**: moody, eerie, dreamlike.
- A little gore is fine; never over the top.
- Intensity varies by product: tee packaging and core merch stay subtler,
  limited drops go darker.

## Palette

Grey with Beksiński's dusty earth tones, plus one accent.

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Base | Ash grey, bone |
| Earth | Ochre, rust, sepia |
| Shadow | Deep umber, near-black |
| Accent | **Teal**, used sparingly. Replaced the earlier acid green. |

## Type

- **Headings and wordmark:** Pirata One, a blackletter whose flared, hooked F
  matches the torn F in the logo.
- **Body:** Spectral. **Captions:** IM Fell English italic. **Labels:** DM Mono.

## Website

Dark ground (near-black umber), bone text, ochre for small labels, teal for the
one call to action. The logo sits on its aged-paper background like a specimen
plate hung on a dark wall.

## Logo

A golf ball, flayed: its cover cut in an X, the skin flaps stretched along an
X-shaped wooden flaying board and nailed at the ends. In the center, an F-shaped
wound in the muscle shows a glowing teal core. Drawn as an old anatomical
engraving.

- **Reference image:** [`logo-reference.png`](logo-reference.png). AI-generated,
  with the bottom nails hand-corrected to match the top ones. It's a reference
  for the illustrator, not the final logo: purely AI-generated images generally
  can't be copyrighted, so the final mark must be drawn by a person.
- **Next step:** commission an illustrator. Look at fine-line and blackwork
  tattoo artists first. The brief is in [`logo-brief.md`](logo-brief.md).
- **Caution:** an X-shaped board with a flayed body is the core of House
  Bolton's sigil in Game of Thrones. Keep it clearly a golf ball, with no human
  figure and no pink or red field, and get a trademark attorney's view before
  printing at volume.
